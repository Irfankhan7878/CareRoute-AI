Department: Orthopaedics & Musculoskeletal Health (Code: ORTHO)
Location: Building B, 2nd Floor, West Wing
Operating Hours: Monday - Friday, 08:30 AM - 05:30 PM; Saturday, 09:00 AM - 01:00 PM
Hotline: Ext. 2020

1. Department Description & Services
The Department of Orthopaedics evaluates and treats acute and subacute musculoskeletal conditions including knee joint pain, osteoarthritis, shoulder impingement, sports-related ligament and meniscus injuries, spinal disc disorders, fractures, and tendonitis.

2. Registration & Intake Instructions
Patients presenting with joint swelling, difficulty walking, acute limb sprains, or chronic back stiffness should check in at Reception Desk B-2. Wheelchair and mobility assistance is available immediately upon arrival at the West Wing ground entrance.

3. Required Documents
- Government photo ID and insurance policy documentation.
- Any prior radiographic films or CDs: X-rays, MRI scans, CT scans, or ultrasound studies of the affected joint.
- Previous orthopaedic consultation summaries, physical therapy progress notes, or surgical discharge notes.
- Current medication record, including non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs) or blood thinners.

4. Preparation Instructions
- Wear loose-fitting, comfortable clothing that allows easy physical examination of the affected joint (e.g., shorts for knee or hip issues, short sleeves for shoulder or elbow evaluations).
- Do not apply topical analgesic ointments, medicated patches, or tight wraps immediately prior to physical examination.
- If walking causes significant pain, request a wheelchair escort from hospital patient transport staff at the main lobby.

5. Department-Specific Intake Notes
Patients with suspected acute fractures, sudden limb deformity, or open bone wounds must be transported directly to the Emergency Trauma Bay rather than outpatient orthopaedics.
