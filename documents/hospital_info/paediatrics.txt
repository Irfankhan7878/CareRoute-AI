Department: Paediatrics & Child Health (Code: PAED)
Location: Children's Pavilion, 1st Floor
Operating Hours: Monday - Saturday, 08:00 AM - 06:00 PM; Urgent Walk-In: 24/7
Hotline: Ext. 2090

1. Department Description & Services
The Paediatrics Department provides specialized medical care for newborns, infants, children, and young adolescents under 18 years of age. Services encompass routine developmental milestone monitoring, immunization programmes, childhood asthma and allergy management, pediatric infectious diseases, and nutritional assessments.

2. Registration & Intake Instructions
Parents or legal guardians must check in at the Children's Pavilion Reception Desk. A registered parent or legal guardian must accompany every minor throughout the clinical consultation.

3. Required Documents
- Child's official birth certificate or national health identification card.
- Government-issued photo ID of the accompanying parent or legal guardian.
- Child immunization / vaccination yellow booklet (original or verified copy).
- Previous pediatric growth charts, specialist clinic summaries, or allergy test records.

4. Preparation Instructions
- Bring familiar comfort items (favorite toy, soft blanket, pacifier) to reduce anxiety during physical examination.
- Prepare a brief record of child's feeding schedule, sleep patterns, and exact fever thermometer measurements (including route: axillary, tympanic, oral).
- Have child dressed in two-piece, easily removable clothing to simplify pediatric auscultation and weight checks.

5. Department-Specific Intake Notes
RED FLAGS IN CHILDREN: High fever with lethargy/unresponsiveness, difficulty breathing with chest wall retractions, non-blanching dark purplish rash, or dehydration with dry diapers for over 8 hours requires immediate transfer to Pediatric Emergency.
