1. Department Description & Services
The Department of Gynaecology provides comprehensive clinical care for women's reproductive health across all life stages. Services include preventive Pap smear and HPV screening, evaluation of menstrual irregularities, polycystic ovarian syndrome (PCOS), pelvic pain, prenatal counselling, menopause management, and pelvic floor assessments.

2. Registration & Intake Instructions
Check in at Women's Health Reception Desk on the 3rd Floor. Female chaperones are available upon request for all pelvic and physical examinations.

3. Required Documents
- National ID card and medical health insurance card.
- Prior pelvic or transvaginal ultrasound scan images and formal radiologist reports.
- Previous Pap smear cytology or cervical biopsy reports from the preceding 3 years.
- Medication log, including oral contraceptive pills, hormone replacement therapy (HRT), or thyroid medications.

4. Preparation Instructions
- Note the exact date of the first day of your last menstrual period (LMP) and your typical cycle length.
- Empty your bladder immediately prior to physical pelvic examination, UNLESS a full bladder is specifically requested for a transabdominal pelvic ultrasound.
- Routine Pap smear tests are best scheduled when not actively menstruating (ideally mid-cycle).

5. Department-Specific Intake Notes
Severe, sudden unilateral pelvic pain, heavy vaginal bleeding soaking more than two large sanitary pads per hour, or suspected ectopic pregnancy requires immediate evaluation at the Emergency Room.
