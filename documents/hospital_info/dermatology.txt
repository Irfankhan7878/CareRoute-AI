1. Department Description & Services
The Dermatology Department diagnoses and manages skin diseases, eczema, psoriasis, cutaneous allergic reactions, acne, dermatitis, pigmentary disorders, alopecia (hair loss), and suspicious mole screening via digital dermoscopy.

2. Registration & Intake Instructions
Report to Suite 210 check-in kiosk. Please inform triage staff if you suspect a contagious rash (e.g., suspected shingles, varicella, or scabies) so isolated intake seating can be arranged.

3. Required Documents
- Photo identification and insurance documentation.
- Names or packaging of current topical ointments, steroid creams, cosmetic preparations, and systemic dermatological medications (e.g., retinoids, biologics).
- Prior skin biopsy reports or allergy patch test records.

4. Preparation Instructions
- Do not apply heavy cosmetic foundation, concealer, tanning lotion, or colored ointments over skin lesions on the day of consultation.
- If nail changes or discoloration are being examined, remove all nail polish or artificial nails at least 24 hours prior to appointment.
- Bring photographs of the skin condition if the rash flares intermittently or changes appearance.

5. Department-Specific Intake Notes
Severe blistering covering extensive body areas, fever accompanied by peeling skin, or facial angioedema with lip swelling requires emergency triage.
